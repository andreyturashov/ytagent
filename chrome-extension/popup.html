How to Setup Gemini Nano

AIst runs 100% locally on your computer using Chrome's built-in Gemini Nano AI. Follow these 4 quick steps to enable it:

1. Enable Chrome Flags
Type chrome://flags in your browser address bar and enable these 2 flags:
  • #prompt-api-for-gemini-nano → Enabled
  • #optimization-guide-on-device-model → Enabled BypassRequirement
2. Relaunch Chrome
Click the Relaunch button at the bottom right of chrome://flags page.
3. Download On-Device Model
Type chrome://components in address bar, find Optimization Guide On Device Model, and click Check for Update until downloaded.
4. Ready to Chat!
Open AIst on any webpage or YouTube video and start analyzing instantly!
